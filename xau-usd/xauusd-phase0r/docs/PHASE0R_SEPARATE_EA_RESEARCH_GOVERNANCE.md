# Phase 0R Separate EA Research Governance

Phase 0R exists to research new candidate mechanics without changing the current canonical EAs, accepted experts, rejected experts, or measured-cost conclusions.

## Boundary

- Current canonical EAs are out of scope.
- Existing accepted and rejected Phase 0 experts are out of scope.
- Phase 0R candidates begin as draft hypotheses, then may be locked before result-producing validation.
- Observer EAs may write passive telemetry only.
- Paper mode and live mode are not authorized by this lane.

## Cost And Evidence Standard

The promotion metric is net expectancy in R after measured cost. Win rate, absolute dollar PnL, and visual equity-curve shape are secondary and cannot promote a candidate by themselves.

Measured spread assumptions:

- measured_median_spread_points = 50
- measured_p95_spread_points = 75
- measured_max_spread_points = 180

## No Tuning

Do not tune a candidate after seeing results. A failed candidate must remain failed. A new thesis requires a new candidate ID, a new version, and a new locked hypothesis before testing.

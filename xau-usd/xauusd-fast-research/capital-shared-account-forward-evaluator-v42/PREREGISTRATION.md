# V42 Shared-Account Forward Preregistration

## Claim

The unchanged five-specialist Core plus the already frozen V27 satellite family
must produce 3.0 through 4.0 trades per complete weekday on the same prospective
Capital stream while retaining positive stress-adjusted expectancy. V42 is an
evaluator, not another strategy attempt.

## Sealed Stages

Validation uses the exact first 20 full weekdays opened by V27. Confirmation
uses V27's next 20 full weekdays and remains sealed unless V42 validation passes
its research gates. No aggregate Core or combined economics are exposed before
a complete stage and complete causal resolutions exist.

## Economics And Floating Equity

All Core P&L is calculated at the source record's 0.01-lot-equivalent risk.
Base net removes ticket and holding costs; stress net additionally applies the
locked 0.05R slippage charge. R5 P&L and exposure retain V39's causal risk
weight. Satellite P&L is consumed exactly from V27.

Floating equity is marked on every available Capital bid/ask tick from entry
through exit. Round-trip costs are charged at entry for a conservative path.
The evaluator also measures concurrent positions, absolute directional lots,
and leverage-based margin.

## Research Gates

- V27 stage passes its own immutable gates.
- 3.0 through 4.0 combined trades per full weekday.
- Positive base and stress net.
- Base PF at least 1.20 and stress PF at least 1.05.
- At least 50% profitable days.
- Base PF at least 1.0 in each chronological half.
- Closed-trade drawdown no more than USD 1,000.

Failure is terminal for this version. Same-version threshold tuning is forbidden.

## Account Readiness

The frozen reference is USD 3,000 equity, 1:100 leverage, 100 ounces per lot,
and a 0.01 minimum/step. Account readiness additionally requires:

- floating equity drawdown no more than 15%;
- worst daily stress loss no more than 3%;
- margin no more than 40%;
- no more than 12 concurrent positions or 0.12 absolute directional lots;
- the historical USD 1,733.37 conservative Core drawdown to fit within 15%;
- an explicitly preregistered broker-lot mapping for R5 fractional weights.

These account gates are deliberately separate from the research claim. With
USD 3,000 equity, the historical Core drawdown gate is known to fail at lock:
USD 1,733.37 / 15% requires at least USD 11,555.80 before adding new risk.

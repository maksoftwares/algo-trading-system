# V100 Causal Health-Gated Event Sleeves Preregistration

## Final-version scope

V100 is the final strategy-search version authorized by the program ceiling.
There is no V101. If V100 does not pass the complete two-trades-per-weekday
shared-account gate, strategy versioning stops and the next artifact is an
evidence-based brainstorming report.

V99 showed that a nonlinear ranker over generic event actions had neither edge
nor sufficient selected frequency. V100 tests a different mechanism: two
previously defined, episode-independent deterministic event families are
scheduled mechanically, while a causal rolling health circuit can temporarily
disable a sleeve. It does not rank or predict individual trades.

## Locked policy registry

Attempts `131001-132000` are the Cartesian product of:

- five fixed family/horizon pools;
- five eight-hour UTC scheduling windows anchored at 00, 04, 08, 12, or 16;
- four health lookbacks of 20, 40, 80, or 160 completed events;
- five activation PF thresholds from 0.9 through 1.3; and
- two cooldowns of 5 or 20 calendar days.

This is exactly 1,000 policies. Each policy takes at most two entries per UTC
date, at most one per family, and at most two concurrent positions. The health
statistic uses only event outcomes whose fixed-horizon exit is strictly before
the candidate entry. A current or future outcome can never affect admission.

## Known contamination

The V19 markout history and V21 results were already exposed during earlier
research. V100 is therefore development evidence, not a pristine independent
test and not trading authorization. The locked Discovery, Confirmation, and
Final progression prevents additional same-version adaptation but cannot undo
earlier program-level exposure.

## Success

A standalone stage pass is not program success. A Final survivor must pass the
frozen shared-account audit with byte-identical V59/V60 and at least 2.0
combined trades per weekday separately in Development-2, Confirmation, and
Final. Combined PF, add-on PF, winner-removal, daily correlation, concurrent
risk, and buffered floating-equity drawdown gates must all pass.

No model fitting, EA, MT5 demo, live action, paid data, Databento, payment, or
broker authority is granted.

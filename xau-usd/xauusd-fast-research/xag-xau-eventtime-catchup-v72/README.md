# XAG to XAU Event-Time Catch-Up V72

V72 tests whether a sharp, causally observed Dukascopy XAGUSD quote move can
identify a temporarily lagging XAUUSD quote and support an additive gold trade.
The first event per eligible UTC date is used. Calibration may select candidate
density only; all P/L remains sealed until the contract is locked.

The lane is historical research only. It cannot authorize Python predictions,
EA consumption, demo trading, live trading, broker action, or paid data.

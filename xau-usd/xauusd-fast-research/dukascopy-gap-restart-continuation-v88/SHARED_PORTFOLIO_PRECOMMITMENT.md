# V88 Shared-Portfolio Precommitment

Only a V88 specialist that passes every standalone stage unchanged may be added
to byte-identical V59/V60. Every required modern window must reach at least two
accepted, broker-executable trades per eligible weekday after conflicts and
risk controls.

The combined portfolio must retain base PF at least 1.50, stress PF at least
1.35, at least 60% positive months, positive winner-removed stress P&L, closed
drawdown no more than USD 500, buffered stress floating drawdown no more than
USD 600, worst-day and worst-five-day losses no more than USD 150 and USD 250,
and absolute daily P&L correlation no more than 0.50.

Blocked, overlapping, delayed, or risk-rejected entries do not count toward
frequency. The audit must model one account and all V60 controls.

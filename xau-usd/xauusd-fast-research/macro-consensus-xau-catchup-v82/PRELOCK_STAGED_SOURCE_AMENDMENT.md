# V82 Prelock Staged-Source Amendment

Date: `2026-07-21`

This amendment exists before calibration or economic outcomes are opened.
Source evidence is audited separately for calibration, development,
confirmation, validation, exam, forward confirmation, and forward final.

- Calibration: January 2019.
- Development: February 2019 through June 2021.
- Confirmation: July 2021 through June 2022.
- Validation: July 2022 through June 2023.
- Exam: July 2023 through June 2024.
- Forward confirmation: July 2024 through June 2025.
- Forward final: July 2025 through June 2026.

Calibration and development source audits may be created before economics.
Every later source audit remains inaccessible until the previous economic stage
passes. The full-source audit remains inaccessible until forward final passes.
This prevents later raw files or their metadata from becoming an informal
selection channel.

No paid source is authorized. Missing later free Dukascopy months may be
acquired only after the preceding stage passes and before that stage's source
audit is sealed.

# One-Trade-Per-Day Health Portfolio V55

V55 is the terminal soft-risk successor to V54. It restores execution of the
unchanged add-on signals while reducing each new add-on to half risk when the
causal closed-equity drawdown reaches USD 225. Full risk resumes only after the
drawdown recovers to USD 180.

V50, every signal, entry, exit, health gate, risk ceiling, concurrency limit,
evaluation window, and economic gate remain unchanged.

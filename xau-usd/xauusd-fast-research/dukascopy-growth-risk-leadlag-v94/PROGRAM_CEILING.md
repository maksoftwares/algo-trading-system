# Frequency Program Ceiling

The frequency research program has a hard ceiling at V100.

- V93 through V100 may test only materially distinct, preregistered hypotheses.
- Success means at least `2.0` combined trades per weekday separately in
  Development-2, Confirmation, and Final after shared-account routing, while all
  registered edge, correlation, cost, and drawdown gates pass.
- V60's existing `>=1.0/day` result cannot be reported as progress toward this
  target.
- A failure cannot be rescued by changing its direction, session, threshold,
  exit, or risk geometry on exposed outcomes.
- If no campaign passes by V100, strategy iteration stops. The next artifact
  must be an evidence-based brainstorming and decision review; V101 is not
  authorized by this program.

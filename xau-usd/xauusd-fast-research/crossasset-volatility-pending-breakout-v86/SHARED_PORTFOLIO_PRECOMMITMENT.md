# V86 Shared-Portfolio Precommitment

Date: `2026-07-21`

Only an unchanged V86 specialist that passes every standalone stage may be
added to byte-identical V59/V60. Every aligned required window must reach at
least 2.0 accepted trades per weekday, base PF 1.50, stress PF 1.35, 60%
positive months, and positive stressed net after removing five winners.

Shared closed drawdown may not exceed USD 500 and buffered stressed floating
drawdown may not exceed USD 600. Worst-day and rolling-five-day stressed losses
may not exceed USD 150 and USD 250. Absolute daily P&L correlation with V59/V60
may not exceed 0.50. Conflicted or risk-blocked entries do not count.

The simulation must preserve chronology, overlap, concurrency, directional
exposure, spread, slippage, commission, holding cost, and all V60 controls.

## [Archived News Releases](https://www.bls.gov/bls/news-release/home.htm) | Producer Price Index

The Producer Price Index (PPI) news release presents data from the [Producer Price Index](https://www.bls.gov/ppi/) program.

#### Caution

Data in archived news releases may have been revised in subsequent releases. The latest data, including any revisions, may be obtained from the databases accessible on the program homepage:

*   [Producer Price Index: databases](https://www.bls.gov/ppi/#data)

# V83 Shared-Portfolio Precommitment

Date: `2026-07-21`

Only a V83 specialist that passes every standalone stage unchanged may be added
to byte-identical V59/V60. On every aligned required window the resulting
single-account timeline must produce at least 2.0 accepted trades per weekday,
base PF at least 1.50, stressed PF at least 1.35, at least 60% positive months,
and positive stressed net after removing its five largest winners.

Shared closed drawdown may not exceed USD 500 and buffered stressed floating
drawdown may not exceed USD 600. Worst-day and rolling-five-day stressed losses
may not exceed USD 150 and USD 250. Absolute daily P&L correlation with V59/V60
may not exceed 0.50. Conflicted or risk-blocked entries do not count as trades.

The simulation must preserve chronology, overlap, concurrency, directional
exposure, spread, slippage, commission, holding cost, and all V60 controls.

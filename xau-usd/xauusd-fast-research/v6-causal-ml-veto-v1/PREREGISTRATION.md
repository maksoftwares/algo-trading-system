# Preregistration

## Question

Can a conservative, causal ML veto preserve useful V6 confirmed-impulse trades
while reducing weak trades, drawdown, and winner concentration relative to the
frozen raw V6 result?

## Frozen Inputs

- The V6 V1 candidate ledger, accepted ledger, implementation, contract, and
  failed result are immutable, source-hashed inputs.
- V60 remains immutable.
- The training population is the unique `BOTH/all/ALL` confirmed-impulse
  population generated by the hashed external V6 mechanism.
- Capital bid/ask outcomes and the V6 V1 cost stress define labels.

## Causal Features

Only information complete before entry is permitted:

- confirmation speed;
- signed flow and book imbalance;
- activity, spread, and price efficiency;
- pre-confirmation adverse movement;
- trend alignment;
- the frozen upstream ranker score;
- prior two-hour movement;
- lagged macro slope;
- log stop distance;
- direction;
- cyclical UTC time;
- contemporaneous causal regime class.

Entry/exit prices, returns, labels, P&L, holding duration, exit time, and future
regime information are forbidden.

## Annual Walk-Forward

For target year `Y`:

- fit rows must have exited before `Y-1-01-01` minus 48 hours;
- calibration rows enter during year `Y-1` and must exit before `Y-01-01`
  minus 48 hours;
- calibration labels cannot choose the cutoff;
- cutoff is the 40th percentile of calibration prediction scores, retaining
  the top 60%;
- the model is not retrained after seeing calibration or target outcomes.

The classifier is one locked shallow histogram gradient-boosted model with
day-equal sample weights. There is no model search in this version.

## Required Improvement

The ML veto passes only if:

- mean annual target AUC is at least 0.55;
- at least three of five target years have AUC above 0.50;
- each required window has at least 10 accepted ML trades;
- ML V6 PF is no worse than raw V6 PF in every required window;
- ML V6 closed drawdown is no worse than raw V6 drawdown in every window;
- ML V6 remains profitable after removing its five largest winners in every
  window;
- V60 plus ML has positive incremental stress P&L in every window;
- V60 plus ML has PF no worse than V60 alone in every window;
- V60 plus ML has closed drawdown no worse than V60 alone in every window;
- full-history combined PF, closed drawdown, and M5 floating drawdown are no
  worse than V60 alone;
- inherited add-on position and initial-risk limits remain satisfied.

## Interpretation

A failure quarantines this exact model and cutoff. A historical pass would only
nominate a prospective shadow research lane; it would not authorize trading.

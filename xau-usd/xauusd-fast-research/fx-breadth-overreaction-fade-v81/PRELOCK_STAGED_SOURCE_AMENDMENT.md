# V81 Prelock Staged-Source Amendment

Date: `2026-07-20`

At this amendment, no post-calibration entry, exit, label, markout, P&L, or
economic gate result has been opened. The outcome-blind calibration policy is

## Purpose

The original packet required all raw source through June 2024 before opening
development. GBPUSD is a new V81 input and its free official Dukascopy source is
slow to acquire. Downloading every later holdout before knowing whether
development passes adds time but no statistical protection.

V81 therefore freezes source in exact economic-stage slices:

1. `development`: September 2018 through June 2021.
2. `confirmation`: July 2021 through June 2022, only after development passes.
3. `validation`: July 2022 through June 2023, only after confirmation passes.
4. `exam`: July 2023 through June 2024, only after validation passes.
5. `forward_confirmation`: July 2024 through June 2025, only after exam passes.
6. `forward_final`: July 2025 through June 2026, only after forward confirmation
   passes.

The immutable strategy contract records the calibration and development source
audits plus every code, policy, execution, cost, gate, and dependency hash.
Later source audits are generated by that already-locked code and must match
their exact registered month bounds before their stage can run. The raw tick
store continues to verify every frozen manifest and hourly byte hash at runtime.

After a forward-final pass, a complete July 2018 through June 2026 source audit
is required before shared-account combination. A failed stage remains
terminal; later economic stages stay sealed. The two forward stages align with
the frozen V59/V60 confirmation and final windows so the combined frequency and
drawdown claim can be tested on the same dates.

This amendment does not change candidate density, direction, timing, sizing,
stops, targets, costs, drawdown limits, significance tests, or any economic
acceptance gate.

# XAUUSD M15 Regime Anti-Signal Campaign V3 Preregistration

## Question

Do broad M15 mechanics that lost consistently in V2 become economically useful when their direction is inverted and their destination is a fixed R target?

This design is informed by already opened historical outcomes. It is discovery evidence and cannot be an independent holdout.

## Changed mechanics

The campaign does not retune V2 mean targets. It tests a different behavioral proposition:

- continuation away from UTC-day VWAP and rolling means while H4 is labeled chop;
- continuation through Asian and prior-day edges;
- continuation in the original sweep direction after a failed fade trigger;
- fade of post-chop breakouts, trend-reactivation signals, and Asian-session expansion during transition;
- fade of extreme post-chop momentum and trend-ancestry exhaustion.

Every definition has a fixed R target frozen at signal close. `UNSAFE_SHOCK` remains ineligible.

## Locked attempts

- Attempts 19120 through 20119 inclusive.
- 500 chop and 500 transition definitions.
- Five mechanics per owner and 100 deterministic hash-selected definitions per mechanic.
- Same-version changes after outcomes are forbidden.

## Execution and gates

The clock-corrected nanosecond M15 engine is mandatory. Entry is next-bar side-correct bid/ask. Stops and targets use executable sides; stop wins same-bar ambiguity. Spread, ticket, holding, and 0.05R stress costs apply.

The V2 economic gates remain unchanged: at least 120 total trades, 15 each era, all-era PF at least 1.10, all-era average at least 0.02R, total PF at least 1.25, drawdown no more than 30R, and positive net after removing five winners.

Any historical survivor requires raw-tick replay and prospective shadow evidence. No result authorizes training or execution.

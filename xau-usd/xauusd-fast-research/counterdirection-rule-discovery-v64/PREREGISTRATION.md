# V64 Counter-Direction Rule Discovery

V64 examines only broker-stressed actions opposite to the original mechanical
signal. It excludes every timestamp already qualified by a V57 sleeve and uses a
bounded width-one through width-four categorical rule census. Each rule must
contain the action horizon and pass both development 2 and confirmation before
the final-year outcomes are loaded by a later locked evaluator.

The final year is already exposed elsewhere in the research program, so a later
success will be historical evidence rather than a pristine holdout. V64 cannot
authorize execution.

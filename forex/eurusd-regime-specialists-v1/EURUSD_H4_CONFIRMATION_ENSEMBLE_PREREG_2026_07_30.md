# EURUSD H4 confirmation ensemble preregistration

The protected baseline is the frozen H4/M15 first-break portfolio, which
already passed every frozen chronological, cost, concentration, delay, and
bootstrap gate but trades only 0.271 times per FX day.

This experiment adds two mechanically different completed-M15 confirmations
in both owned quiet regimes: an immediate next close beyond the same
00:00-06:00 UTC range and a touch-plus-rejection within four completed M15
bars. All four confirmation experts are mandatory. No favorable expert,
regime, year, or subgroup may be deleted after the combined result.

The signal side remains SHORT. Stops, targets, maximum hold, bid/ask execution,
slippage, stop-first same-bar policy, H4 regime availability, and quarantine
remain inherited from the frozen parent. Chop uses one risk unit and
compression uses half a unit. A fixed two-unit portfolio cap prioritizes the
protected core, then next-close, then retest experts.

The exact ensemble must reach at least 0.35 trades per FX day while retaining
45%-55% wins, 1.35-1.75 realized payoff, full PF at least 1.15, 0.5-pip
stressed PF at least 1.10, PF above 1 in all four chronological blocks,
recent and latest-12-month PF at least 1.20, best-5%-removed PF at least 1,
drawdown no greater than 18R, and both trade- and calendar-block bootstrap
lower-tail gates.

This is adaptive historical development, not pristine forward evidence. A
historical pass identifies a stronger backtest candidate but cannot authorize
broker orders.

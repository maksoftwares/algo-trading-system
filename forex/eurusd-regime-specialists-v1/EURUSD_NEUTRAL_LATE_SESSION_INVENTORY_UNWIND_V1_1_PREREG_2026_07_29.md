It did not open a post-entry path, return, P&L, oracle row, win rate, or
profitability statistic. Its 8-pip member retained 46 candidates and failed
the unchanged capacity gates. V1.1 is therefore an explicitly adaptive
source-only capacity successor, not a performance repair.

confirmation, 1.5-pip confirmation floor, 15% retracement, 00:15 entry,

## Final capacity ladder

The displacement thresholds are permanently ordered `6`, then `4` pips.
The first member passing every unchanged capacity gate is selected. Neither
candidate count nor later evidence may push the floor below four pips. If
neither passes, the mechanism is closed without outcomes.

## Boundary

A passing census permits only a separately implemented and hash-locked
execution. Historical profitability cannot authorize demo or live trading,
and this code has no broker capability.

# EURUSD M15 regime-portfolio forward adjudication protocol

Frozen before the `2026-08-01 00:00 UTC` forward floor.

This protocol converts only new demo-shadow signals into an immutable outcome
ledger. Historical tester trades and pre-floor signals are forbidden.

## Inputs

The signal input is the append-only
`EURUSD_M15_REGIME_PORTFOLIO_SHADOW_DEMO.csv` ledger from demo account
`1033669 / Capital.ComMena-Demo`. Every accepted signal must:

- use run `EURUSD_M15_REGIME_FORWARD_V1`;
- be EURUSD, SHORT, and either CHOP or COMPRESSION;
- occur on a 00/15/30/45 minute boundary from 06:00 through 09:59 UTC;
- be followed by a matching `ORDER_BLOCKED=shadow_or_orders_disabled` row; and
- use the frozen 0.02 chop or 0.01 compression size.

The path input is the independent prospective collector's EURUSD M5
`PROSPECTIVE_DEMO` bid/ask ledger. Tester rows, missing intervals, non-OK
sources, and incomplete two-sided quotes are not imputed.

## Outcome

The audit signal supplies the entry, stop, and target generated by the frozen
EA. From the exact signal interval onward, each complete M5 ask bar is checked
stop first, then target. Gaps fill at the first ask. If neither boundary is
reached, the position exits at the last ask of the 144th complete M5 interval,
representing the unchanged 12-hour hold. A separate 0.5-pip round-trip stress
is reported.

Only terminal RESOLVED or INVALID outcomes enter the ledger. Still-open paths
remain pending outside it, so a prior row never changes. All writes are atomic,
and reruns refuse deletion or mutation of any existing outcome.

## Admission

The pooled, unchanged chop-plus-compression portfolio requires:

- at least 50 resolved trades over at least 240 calendar days;
- at least eight active months;
- base PF at least 1.15 and stressed PF at least 1.05;
- PF at least 1.0 after removing the best 5% of trades;
- PF above 1.0 in both chronological trade-sequence halves;
- at least ten trades in each regime and component PF at least 0.75;
- no month above 50% of gross profit;
- zero invalid outcomes;
- exact MT5 signal parity; and
- a completed demo shadow soak.

Compression cannot be deleted or reweighted after its outcome is visible.
Passing this protocol still does not authorize orders. Final authorization also
requires the combined daily-frequency portfolio, execution parity, and risk
gates.

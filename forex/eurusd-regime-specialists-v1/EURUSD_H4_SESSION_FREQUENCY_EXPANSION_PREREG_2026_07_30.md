# EURUSD H4 session-frequency expansion preregistration

The protected baseline remains the unchanged H4 chop expert at 1.0 risk plus
the unchanged H4 compression expert at 0.5 risk.

Before opening the transferred-session outcomes, this experiment mechanically
copies those two experts to:

- New York: completed 06:00-11:00 UTC reference and 12:00-15:00 decisions;
- late US: completed 12:00-17:00 UTC reference and 18:00-21:00 decisions.

Only the clock changes. Regime ownership, SHORT direction, body threshold,
stop, target, 12-hour hold, first signal per session date, bid/ask execution,
spread floor/ceiling, slippage, stop-first policy, quarantine, and exact M5
path remain unchanged.

Each transferred session is one inseparable chop-plus-compression bundle. A
bundle must pass every frozen standalone admission gate; individual regimes,
years, or attractive subgroups cannot be selected. Every passing bundle must
be added at half the baseline session risk. A causal 2.0R concurrent-risk cap
is applied using the fixed priority in the configuration.

Success requires at least 50% more accepted trades than the 507-trade baseline
while preserving full PF 1.15, +0.5-pip PF 1.10, positive +1-pip and delayed
entry scenarios, positive frozen chronological blocks, positive latest-six-
month net, PF at least 1.15 in the latest year, best-5%-removed PF at least
1.0, drawdown at most 18R, and both trade- and calendar-block bootstrap lower
bounds above PF 1.

This is selection-aware historical development, not pristine out-of-sample
evidence. No result can authorize demo or broker activity.

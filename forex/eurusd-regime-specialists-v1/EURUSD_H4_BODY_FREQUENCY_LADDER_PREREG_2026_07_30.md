# EURUSD H4 body-filter frequency ladder preregistration

The profitable session, regimes, SHORT direction, reference range, decision
hours, stop, target, hold, and execution stay unchanged. Only the completed
H1 breakout candle's minimum body fraction is widened through four fixed
levels:

- protected: chop 0.35, compression 0.55;
- moderate: chop 0.25, compression 0.45;
- broad: chop 0.15, compression 0.35;
- all breaks: both 0.00.

The levels are evaluated as complete chop-plus-compression portfolios at the
protected 1.0/0.5 weights. Crossed threshold pairs and interpolation after
outcomes are forbidden.

A nonbaseline level must add at least 20% more trades and pass every frozen
full-history, chronological, recent, cost, delay, concentration, drawdown,
and bootstrap gate. The broadest passing level is selected. If none passes,
the original thresholds remain protected.

This is historical development, not pristine confirmation, and no result can
authorize demo or broker activity.
